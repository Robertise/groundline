Get started

  1. Create a campaign. Describe who you want to reach and paste a few X handles or seed accounts.
  2. Run discovery. The app sets aside people who don't fit or can't be reached, scores the rest, and shows the quotes behind each score.
  3. Draft, review, approve. Drafts use only verified quotes. Nothing is sent until you approve the exact text.
  4. Send and track on the Outreach page.
